📐 Laws of Exponents

Product Rule

am × an = am+n

Example: 2³ × 2⁴ = 2⁷ = 128

Quotient Rule

am ÷ an = am−n

Example: 5⁶ ÷ 5² = 5⁴ = 625

Power Rule

(am)n = am×n

Example: (3²)³ = 3⁶ = 729

Zero Exponent

a0 = 1

Example: 99⁰ = 1

Negative Exponent

a−n = 1 / an

Example: 2⁻³ = 1/8 = 0.125

Power of Product

(ab)n = an × bn

Example: (2×3)² = 4 × 9 = 36

Power of Quotient

(a/b)n = an / bn

Example: (4/2)³ = 64/8 = 8

Fractional Exponent

a1/n = ⁿ√a

Example: 271/3 = ³√27 = 3
